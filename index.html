Option 1, how it works: the price difference × days left in the cycle ÷ days in the cycle. Days left run from the change date up to the day before the next billing date.

A negative result is a downgrade. Refund it, or credit it against their next invoice. From their usual billing date they pay the new plan's full price.

If the new plan includes more hours, give them the same share of the extra hours (days left ÷ days in the cycle).

Option 2, how it works: the current plan's price × unused days ÷ days in the cycle is refunded. The new plan is charged in full on the change date and every month after on that same day.

If the client uses both plans' hours on the change date, leave the tick on so that day isn't refunded.

Move their billing date and cancel the old plan's next charge, or they will be billed twice.
